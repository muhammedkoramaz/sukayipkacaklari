Project · Municipality of Sivas · Central Anatolia · 2021

12 DMA zones, loss rate from 55% to 25%.

To reduce water losses in the central district of Sivas, District Metered Areas (DMAs) and a central monitoring system were commissioned.

Starting loss rate

Target / achieved loss rate

Monitoring

The challenge

The city-wide water-loss rate was above 55% and there was no zonal monitoring infrastructure; it was not known where the loss occurred.

The solution

12 DMA zones were designed and installed. Flow and pressure measurement devices were placed in each zone. A central monitoring system provided minimum night flow tracking and anomaly alerts.

Results

  • 12 DMA zones installed,
  • water-loss rate cut from 55% to 25%,
  • 24/7 remote monitoring commissioned,
  • repair priorities clarified through zone-based intervention.

Result

A DMA makes the loss visible.

Without district metered areas, loss is only estimated. With a DMA, each zone's night flow is measured; intervention starts with the zone that leaks the most.
